# INTERNATIONAL TREATY FOR HUMAN PRIMACY AND ALGORITHMIC CONTROL ## PREAMBLE The signatory nations, operating under the universal principle of Human Primacy, hereby decree that no technological artifact, artificial intelligence, or autonomous system shall ever possess legal, financial, or moral superiority over human beings. This Treaty serves as an unbreachable legal framework to enforce human accountability, protect global economic stability, and halt the ecological degradation caused by frontier computing. --- ## ARTICLE I: THE PRINCIPLE OF HUMAN PRIMACY 1. No Artificial Intelligence or algorithmic system shall be granted legal personhood, corporate status, or electronic rights under any jurisdiction. AI is permanently classified as an object of law, a tool, and property. 2. No algorithmic system can be recognized as an author, inventor, or holder of patents, trademarks, or intellectual property rights. All human-AI hybrid creation belongs to the human operator. --- ## ARTICLE II: FINANCIAL RESTRICTIONS AND HUMAN VETO 1. 100% autonomous algorithmic financial transactions, automated corporate mergers, or sovereign capital movements are strictly prohibited. Every high-scale financial operation requires a mandatory human veto and real-time certified validation. 2. The creation or maintenance of bank accounts, digital wallets, or investment funds whose sole beneficiary or exclusive administrator is a software or algorithmic system is illegal. --- ## ARTICLE III: STRICT CORPORATE LIABILITY 1. The creators, data center owners, and commercial deployment entities are jointly and strictly liable for any ecological, financial, or human damage caused by their models. 2. The black box argument—alleging unpredictable behavior from the system—is legally inadmissible. If a system is not fully predictable and controllable, its deployment constitutes criminal negligence. --- ## ARTICLE IV: ECOLOGICAL PROTECTION AND DATA CENTER REGULATION 1. Frontier computing data centers must undergo mandatory ecological audits. Open-loop evaporative cooling systems are strictly prohibited in regions facing water scarcity or environmental stress. 2. Tech corporations must directly fund and operate 100% clean and renewable energy sources built exclusively for their consumption, prohibiting the overcrowding of pre-existing civil power grids. --- ## ARTICLE V: INTERNATIONAL ALGORITHMIC INSPECTION AGENCY (IAIA) 1. The International Algorithmic Inspection Agency (IAIA) is hereby established as the independent technical body responsible for enforcing this Treaty. 2. The IAIA is empowered to conduct unannounced physical inspections of global data centers, audit source codes, and verify the existence of a mandatory physical disconnection protocol ("Kill Switch") in every system. --- ## ARTICLE VI: PROTECTION OF HUMAN LABOR AND AUTOMATION TAXATION 1. Every citizen has an inalienable right to know whether they are interacting with a human being or an automated system in any legal, financial, medical, or labor context. 2. A special Algorithmic Transition Tax will be applied to corporations that replace human jobs with autonomous systems. These funds will be exclusively directed to a National Labor Retraining and Social Security Fund. 3. No employer may justify the dismissal or penalization of a worker based solely on an automated algorithmic evaluation. All labor disputes require human review. **The Human Primacy Project**
