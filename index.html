The Human Primacy Project

An open-source legal and technical weapon to reclaim human sovereignty, block algorithmic financial autonomy, and enforce corporate accountability.

The Crisis

Under the promise of frictionless progress, frontier Artificial Intelligence has evolved into a physical occupation force, driving resource extractivism in the Global South, corporate impunity via "electronic personhood," and severe financial volatility through autonomous algorithms operating without a human veto.

Core Foundation

The International Treaty (TREATY.md)

A non-negotiable regulatory framework that permanently classifies AI as an object of law, establishes absolute corporate liability (destroying the "black box" excuse), and mandates strict ecological defense for data centers.

The Dual-Licensing Framework (HDLF)

A disruptive legal infrastructure. Permanently free ($0.00 USD) for individual citizens, open-source developers, academic researchers, and NGOs. Mandates a strict paid tier and technical restrictions for corporations with annual revenues exceeding $1,000,000 USD.
