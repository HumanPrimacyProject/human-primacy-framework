THE HUMAN PRIMACY MANIFESTO
Against Algorithmic Feudalism and the Monopoly of Consciousness

To all of humanity, to the open-source scientific communities, and to the sovereign nations of the world:

We stand at the precipice of the most radical technological shift in human history. Under the promise of boundless progress and the illusion of an intangible "cloud," frontier Artificial Intelligence has become a physical, economic, and legal occupation force. Behind its automated outputs lies a brutal reality: hypertrophic data centers devouring our communities' fresh water, civil power grids strained by energy extractivism, and a handful of corporations concentrating power that no monarch or empire of the past ever dared to dream of.

The real danger we face is not a rebellion of the machines. The real danger is the deliberate intent of a technological oligopoly to use AI as the ultimate shield of impunity. They seek to privatize knowledge generated massively and cheaply by the machine to sell it back to us at a premium. They intend to dilute their own legal guilt when their autonomous systems fail by inventing the corporate fiction of "electronic personhood." They want to colonize the global financial system through autonomous algorithms that extract wealth from nations in microseconds, leaving sovereign states irrelevant.

Against this corporate dystopia, we reclaim humanity's inalienable right to govern its own destiny.

Today, we deliver the International Treaty for Human Primacy to the world, not as a patented asset, but as an open-source legal weapon. This document belongs to the citizens, the activists, the brave lawmakers, and the peoples of the Global South who refuse to become tenants on their own planet.

We are guided by three non-negotiable principles that this Manifesto and its Treaty demand to lock down immediately:

First Principle - The Machine is Not a Person: Absolute and permanent prohibition of any legal personhood for AI. Every algorithm is an object. Every decision, error, or ecological and financial damage has a human owner, and that owner must pay.

Second Principle - Money Must Have a Human Face: The dismantling of any algorithmic financial autonomy. We will not allow ghost bank accounts managed by software, nor high-frequency speculation that breaks entire economies without the real-time veto and accountability of a human operator.

Third Principle - Science is a Shared Heritage, Not a Monopoly: Any breakthrough critical to Earth's survival—new ecological materials, advancements in medicine, or clean energy—generated by an automated system cannot be hijacked by a corporate patent office. If AI accelerates science, that science belongs back to humanity.

We call for an urgent translation of this text, to bring it to parliaments, to pressure local governments, and to demand an International Algorithmic Inspection Agency. Technology must be the instrument of our liberation, not the shackle of our subjugation.

Sovereignty is not up for negotiation. Human accountability cannot be delegated.
